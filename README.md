**START.EXE** is your go-to interactive guide for navigating the exciting world of project development, from your very first line of code to deploying your 'garbage' (or genius!) web app! Ever wondered how to get started, make your projects GitHub-worthy, or even host them online? This tool is built to demystify the process and get you building, breaking, and deploying like a pro! 🚀

## 🌟 What START.EXE Brings to Your Dev Journey!

START.EXE is packed with features designed to kickstart and accelerate your development skills:

*   **The Infinite Dev Loop:** Master the essential cycle of `Learn → Build → Break → Fix → Deploy → Repeat` with practical guidance and insights.
*   **Your Personal Roadmap:** Get a clear, step-by-step roadmap, guiding you from absolute beginner (0) to tackling advanced projects. No more guessing where to start!
*   **Spark Your Creativity:** Receive unique, GitHub-worthy project ideas tailored to your current skill level, ensuring you're always challenged and inspired.
*   **Demystify Deployment:** Learn how to host your creations, from simple static sites to dynamic web apps, making your work accessible to the world!
